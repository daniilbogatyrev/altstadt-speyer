2. Overview

We process personal data only as far as necessary to run this website and handle your booking. We do not use tracking, analytics or advertising services, so this website does not ask for cookie consent. Fonts, photos and the review award images are served from our own server; no connection to third parties is made for them.

3. Hosting and server logs

The website is delivered through Cloudflare’s infrastructure (Cloudflare, Inc., 101 Townsend St, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA). When you open the site, technically necessary data is processed briefly, in particular your IP address, date and time, the page requested, and browser and device type. This serves to deliver the site, defend against attacks and fix errors (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). Logs are kept for a few days only. Cloudflare may transfer data to the USA on the basis of the EU-US Data Privacy Framework or standard contractual clauses. For security, Cloudflare may set a technically necessary security cookie (e.g. “__cf_bm”).

4. Storage in your browser (cookies and local storage)

The public website sets no cookies. If you switch the language or the light/dark mode yourself, your browser remembers your choice in local storage so it is kept on your next visit:

  • altstadt-speyer-lang: chosen language
  • altstadt-theme: chosen appearance (light or dark)

This storage is necessary for the convenience you explicitly requested (Section 25(2) no. 2 TDDDG) and contains no personal data. You can delete it in your browser at any time. A login cookie exists only for the landlord in the administration area.

5. Booking request

When you send a booking request through the form, we process your name, email address, phone number (optional), the chosen apartment, travel dates, number of guests, your message (optional) and the language in which you use the site. We use this data to review and confirm your request and to handle the contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). Without name, email address and travel dates we cannot process the request. The data is stored in a database at Cloudflare.

Retention: Rejected or expired requests are deleted automatically after 12 months. Confirmed and paid bookings are kept because of statutory retention duties (Sections 257 HGB, 147 AO; up to ten years; Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR) and deleted afterwards. If you write to us by email we process your details to handle your enquiry and delete them once the purpose has ceased and no retention duty applies.

6. Spam protection (Cloudflare Turnstile)

The request form is protected against automated requests by Cloudflare Turnstile. When the form loads and is submitted, data such as your IP address, browser and device information and interaction signals are sent to Cloudflare (USA) to determine whether the request comes from a human. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in protection against abuse (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). The transfer is based on the EU-US Data Privacy Framework or standard contractual clauses.

7. Email delivery (Resend)

We send confirmations and notifications about your request through the service Resend (Resend, Inc., USA). Your email address, name and booking details are processed for this (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). The transfer to the USA is based on appropriate safeguards, in particular standard contractual clauses.

8. Payment (Stripe)

After we confirm your request you pay through a payment link from Stripe (Stripe Payments Europe, Ltd., 1 Grand Canal Street Lower, Grand Canal Dock, Dublin, Ireland). You enter your payment details directly with Stripe; we do not receive them, only the information whether the payment was made, plus your email address and the amount (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). Stripe partly processes data under its own responsibility, for example for fraud prevention and legal obligations. More: stripe.com/privacy. Data may also be transferred to Stripe, Inc. (USA) on the basis of the EU-US Data Privacy Framework or standard contractual clauses.

9. External links

This website contains links to Booking.com, Airbnb, OpenStreetMap and possibly Google. Merely visiting our site does not transmit data to these providers. Only when you click a link do the privacy rules of the respective provider apply.

10. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right of access (Art. 15), rectification (Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17), restriction of processing (Art. 18), data portability (Art. 20) and to object to processing based on legitimate interests (Art. 21). You can withdraw any consent at any time with effect for the future. Please contact the address given above.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority, for example: Der Landesbeauftragte für den Datenschutz und die Informationsfreiheit Rheinland-Pfalz, Hintere Bleiche 34, 55116 Mainz, www.datenschutz.rlp.de.
